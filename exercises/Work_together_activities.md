# Work together 

### Work together activity - 1


Challange 1: 

The partipants are divided in pairs and will get the first challange that is complementary to the next challange. They need to find the solution, discuss and make notes in their project.

> **How can I travel within the timeline to check for differences in my versions?** 

Use a search-engine to find out how you can compare differences between the current `commit` and a previous `commit`.
 

Challange 2:

In the same pain partipants receive the second challange. They need to find the solution, discuss and make notes in their project.

 > **How can I see the history of my timeline?**

Use a search-engine to find out how you can list the `commits` on your timeline engine.
